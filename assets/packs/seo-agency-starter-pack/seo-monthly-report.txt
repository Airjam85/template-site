REPORTING NOTES
========================================

Data may differ slightly between platforms because each tool uses different attribution methods, update schedules, and measurement systems. Comparisons should use consistent sources and reporting periods whenever possible.

Search rankings may fluctuate due to seasonality, competitor activity, search engine updates, location, device, personalization, and changes to search result layouts.

SEO performance should be evaluated over meaningful periods rather than through isolated daily movements.
